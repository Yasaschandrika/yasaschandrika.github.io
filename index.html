02

SENSING • ENERGY • LTSPICE

Piezoelectric Smart Road System

Energy Harvesting & Traffic Density Detection

A smart-road concept exploring how vehicle pressure can be used for both energy harvesting and traffic-density sensing. The energy-harvesting circuit was designed and simulated in LTspice; the traffic-density and signal-control portion is presented as a proposed system design.

Energy Harvesting

Piezoelectric-source model → bridge rectifier → 4700 µF storage capacitor.

Simulation

200 ms transient analysis with a 1 kΩ load.

Observed Output

Storage-node voltage rises with periodic ripple; peak is approximately 70–72 V in the shown simulation.

Traffic Concept

Pressure pulses → microcontroller counting → density estimate → adaptive signal timing.

Simulation note

The LTspice inputs are sinusoidal voltage-source models representing piezoelectric outputs. This is a circuit-level simulation, not a measurement from a physical road prototype.
